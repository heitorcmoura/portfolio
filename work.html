e-Kanban inventory management for a blood work clinic

Information Systems · Group project

As part of a digital transformation proposal, designed an e-Kanban solution for a blood work clinic moving to a cloud-based inventory management system (IMS). The system automatically triggers stock orders from the central warehouse, replacing the clinic's manual fax ordering, and tracks each delivery in one place, including status updates and back orders.

Deliverable: e-Kanban solution design.

My role: I researched the e-Kanban solution, worked out how to integrate it into the clinic's operations, and described the benefits of implementing it.
