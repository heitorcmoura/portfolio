About me

I'm a business analyst with more than seven years of experience gathering requirements, working with data, and testing systems in the education sector.

In my recent work in educational publishing, I connected product managers, academic clients and development teams. I documented requirements for custom learning platforms, led quality assurance on more than 150 software updates a year, and built tools that tracked product usage to guide a $500,000 budget. Before that, I managed technical projects worth up to $1 million.

I'm currently completing the postgraduate Information Systems Business Analysis (T405) program at George Brown College. My coursework covers process modeling, requirements elicitation and test strategy design.

What I'm looking for

I'd love to work as a business analyst in public education or in technology here in Toronto. I want to help organizations understand what their users need and turn that into systems that work well for everyone.
